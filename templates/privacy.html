Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

Quick Text Toolkit is built with privacy in mind. We do not store, save, or harvest any text, phone numbers, or custom messages processed through our tools. All text transformations and link generations happen dynamically and are not retained on our servers.

2. Third-Party Services & Cookies

We use third-party services such as Google Analytics and Google AdSense to analyze site traffic and serve advertisements. These services may use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to our site or other websites on the internet.

3. Managing Cookies

Users may choose to set their web browser to refuse cookies or to alert you when cookies are being sent. If you do so, note that some parts of the site may not function properly.

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, feel free to reach out to us via our site options.
