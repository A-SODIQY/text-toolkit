Why Use a Direct WhatsApp Link & QR Generator?

Connecting with customers or audience members should be frictionless. Instead of forcing clients to manually save your phone number into their contacts before sending a message, a direct WhatsApp link allows anyone to open a chat with you in just one click.

Benefits of Direct WhatsApp Links

  • Instant Communication: Customers can reach out directly from your Instagram bio, Facebook page, or site header.
  • Pre-filled Messages: Add customized greeting text (e.g., "Hi, I want to inquire about your services") so users know exactly how to start the conversation.
  • Scannable QR Codes: Print your generated QR code on business cards, posters, or flyers for easy offline-to-online marketing.

How to Create Your Link

  1. Enter your mobile number including your international country code (e.g., 234... or 1... without the + sign).
  2. (Optional) Type a default welcome message.
  3. Click Generate Link & QR Code and copy your custom URL or download the QR image!
